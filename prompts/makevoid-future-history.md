# Claude Predicts the Next 50 Years of Future History

A video by [@makevoid](https://github.com/makevoid) · [Watch the original video](https://youtu.be/fZAgiKb-YOU)

- **Category:** Explainers
- **Author:** [@makevoid](https://github.com/makevoid)

These are the cleaned-up prompts shown on screen in the video. The first prompt establishes the short; the second expands it into the linked long-form video.

## Prompt 1 — the short

```text
Create a 1-minute-20-second vertical video in which Claude speaks directly to the viewer and predicts how AI and robotics will change the world over the next 50 years: economics, world politics, jobs, science, medicine, energy, agriculture, education, home life, information and the internet, self-driving cars and transportation, prices and wealth. Don't search the web — base it on your own knowledge and mention your knowledge cutoff. Make it dramatic and futuristic, open with a strong hook, and name real companies. Style: seinen anime, with Claude as a futuristic robot teacher in a Ghost in the Shell-inspired world, plus a robot pet helper. Start in the future, then show how we got there. At the end, explain the prompt, show it on screen, and point viewers to the long-form video below this post.
```

## Prompt 2 — the long-form video

```text
Plan the long-form video: expand it, produce it to a high standard, animate it, and make it descriptive and effective. Think it through and lay out every option and detail, including for experts in the field, with on-screen overlays that link to recent research papers. You can now use the web, but you can't change the forecast's thesis or trajectory: you can only go deeper in every area and become more certain the predictions were right.
```
